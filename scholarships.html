The Program

Not a prize. A program.

A prize happens once. A program runs all year. Impact awards scholarships at three different points in a founder's story — the cash that gets someone moving, the full year of guidance that gets the business built, and one $50,000 award that changes a life outright.

The Three Scholarships

One program, three ways in.

These aren't competing prizes. They're a beginning, a middle and a summit — because a founder needs something different on day one than they need in year one.

Scholarships are open to everyone

The Rising Leaders Scholarship is open to entrepreneurs anywhere in the United States, 18 and over. You do not need to be an Impact member to apply, and membership never buys preferential access to scholarship money. Impact is a 501(c)(3) — that line matters to us, and we hold it.

Rising Leaders Scholarship

$50,000 for one entrepreneur.

The flagship award. One entrepreneur, once a year, funded to start their business properly — and named live on stage at the Impact Launch Gala.

What it covers

Startup costs and legal fees, plus our mentorship program, the Impact community and every tool needed to launch right.

Who can apply

Entrepreneurs anywhere in the US, 18 or older, with a named business. Finalists pitch in person in San Diego.

This year's cycle

Applications close September 1, 2026. Finalists are chosen by September 5 and pitch September 8–18. The winner is named at the Launch Gala on Saturday, September 26.

Roadmap Scholarship

You bring the calling. We bring the roadmap.

This one doesn't hand anyone a check. It hands them everything it takes to actually build the thing — a full year of it, .

What the recipient receives

  • A full year of Impact membership — round tables, TED Talks, workshops, happy hours, Impact Days, the member forum and monthly calls
  • A seat in a group cohort — building alongside other founders at the same stage
  • Six 1:1 coaching sessions — the plan, the numbers, the paperwork
  • A dedicated mentor for the year — a founder who has already built one, monthly
  • The complete resource library — business plans, financial models, pitch decks and strategy frameworks, ready to use
  • A business spotlight — their business, in front of the room
  • Business formation help — an attorney consultation plus support filing the LLC, the EIN and the San Diego business license
  • A seat at the Impact Launch Gala — in the room with the people who funded them

Why we put a number on it

Every line above has a real market price in San Diego — a cohort seat, an hour of coaching, a year of a recognized founder's time. We add them up honestly and then state less than the total. Anyone who checks the arithmetic will find more support than we claimed. That's the only side of that line worth standing on.

First Step Scholarship

The money that funds the first step.

To know the thing you're supposed to build and have no idea what the first step is — that is where most people stop. The First Step Scholarship is the award that funds that step: $5,000 to $10,000 in cash, quarterly.

Rising Leaders names where someone is going. First Step names where they are. They're a pair, not two competing prizes — a beginning and a summit.

Not open yet — and we'll say so until it is

We won't promise a cycle we can't fund. The First Step Scholarship opens once the money for the first award is in the bank, and every cycle will be announced about a month before applications open. Until then this is what we're building, not what we're taking applications for.

If you want to hear about it first, email info@we-impact.com and we'll tell you the moment a cycle is set.
