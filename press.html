A $50,000 scholarship, awarded live on stage

Through the Rising Leaders Scholarship, Impact awards $50,000 to one entrepreneur as a grant, and the founder keeps full ownership of the business. The finalists pitched to a panel of judges in San Diego on Saturday, October 17. The recipient will be announced live on stage at the Impact Launch Gala.

Funded by membership

Impact is funded by a membership community. Members pay monthly or annual dues, and those dues fund the scholarships, so every member directly supports the founders we back.
