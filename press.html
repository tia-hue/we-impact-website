The part most people have not heard

The money comes from a membership community. Members pay monthly or annual dues, and those dues fund the scholarships. Nobody is asked to donate to a cause. People join something, and the joining is what pays a founder to begin.

“To equip entrepreneurs with the tools and support they need to start and grow sustainable businesses that honor God, fuel economic growth, and create lasting impact in their communities.”
