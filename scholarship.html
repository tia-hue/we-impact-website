The Award

Fifty thousand dollars. Yes — fifty thousand.

Most dreams don't die from a lack of passion. They die in the gap between the calling and the plan — between having the idea and having the money, the paperwork, and someone in your corner who has done it before. This scholarship closes that gap for one entrepreneur, completely.

What the $50,000 covers

This isn't a check and a handshake. It's everything required to open the doors and stay open.

Your startup costs

Equipment, inventory, build-out, licensing, insurance — the real costs of opening, funded.

Legal fees

Entity formation, contracts, trademarks — set up properly from day one, not patched later.

Our mentorship program

You're paired with people who have built businesses and will walk the first year with you.

The Impact community

You grow alongside other founders building at the same time — nobody does this alone.

Every tool you need

Business plan, financial model, systems, and templates — the roadmap, ready to use.

A business built to give back

We help you build something that lifts your community, not just your bottom line.

The Process

You bring your idea. You pitch it to us. That's it.

No complicated forms, no fees, no gatekeeping. Four steps from application to announcement.

01

Apply

Tell us who you are, what you want to build, and what the $50,000 would make possible. Applications are open July 29 through August 24, 2026.

02

Interview

Selected applicants sit down with us. We want to understand your idea, your readiness, and your heart for your community.

03

Pitch to the panel

Finalists present their business to a panel of five judges — founders and business leaders who have built something themselves.

04

The winner is announced

One entrepreneur receives the full award and begins the program with our mentors and community behind them.

Who This Is For

You already know what it is.

You've felt it for years — in your notes app, on napkins, at 11pm scrolling past someone already living the life you keep picturing. Nobody ever handed you the map.

This scholarship is for you if:

  • You have a named business and an idea you believe in, and haven't been able to fund it
  • You're ready to actually launch — not someday, this year
  • You want to build something that gives back to your community, not just to you
  • You're willing to be coached, and to show up for the people coming behind you

Notice what we're not asking for

We're not asking for a perfect deck, a finished product, or a track record. We're asking what you'd build and who it would serve. Philanthropy is one of our core values — it isn't what you do once you've made it, it's built into how you start.

Apply

Ready? Let's hear it.

Applications are open July 29 through August 24, 2026. Send us the five things below and we'll be in touch about interviews.

The application opens right here on the page. It asks serious questions, because $50,000 is a serious award and we're choosing one person out of everyone who applies. Set aside 30–45 minutes and answer it properly — thoughtful beats polished every time.

  • Who you are, and your business name
  • The business — what it is, who buys it, who else does it, what you'll charge and what it costs you
  • Where you are today, and what you've already built with little or no money
  • A real line-by-line breakdown of how you'd spend the $50,000
  • Your break-even math, and how the business sustains itself after the award
  • Licenses and permits you need, and what could kill this in year one
  • Your biggest obstacle — and what happens if you don't win
  • How the business gives back, and why this one is yours to build

You'll also confirm that you'll meet with an Impact mentor monthly and account for how the funds are spent. This award was funded by people who gave — we steward it carefully.
