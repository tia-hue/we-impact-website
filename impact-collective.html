What it is

Grow yours. Help theirs begin.

The Impact Collective is Impact’s new program for founders and artists.

Members grow their businesses while helping the next generation of entrepreneurs begin theirs.

  1. Featured

    on our website and social channels.

  2. Invited

    into our community and events.

  3. Showcased

    at our silent auctions, starting with the Impact Launch Gala on Saturday, November 14, 2026, at the Pendry San Diego.

What guides us

Our values

  1. 01

    Generosity

    We believe generosity and business growth strengthen one another.

  2. 02

    Fairness

    The founders who give are honored as fully as the founders who receive.

  3. 03

    Stewardship

    Every gift is handled with care, and every resource is put to good use.

  4. 04

    Community

    Founders walk alongside one another, for the long run.

  5. 05

    Service

    We lead with love, and with what we can give to the next founder.

Our promise

The Impact Collective is as committed to the success of the founders who give as it is to the success of the founders who receive.

The Collective · November Spotlight

San Diego small business founders

Founder · The Produce Table San Diego

Alex Grillo

Connecting San Diego shoppers with local growers and bringing fresh, seasonal produce closer to their communities. The Produce Table is the app that links shoppers to the growers behind their produce, from field to season.

Founder · Blacktivision Productions San Diego

Tory Robinson

Documenting the moments that matter and helping people and organizations bring their stories to life through film. Blacktivision has captured San Diego’s defining moments on film since 2016.

blacktivision.com ↗

Founder · Breathwork w Ky San Diego

Kylie Cappellano

Kylie is a trauma-informed, Soma+IQ certified breathwork facilitator and a USD law graduate who guides founders and high-achievers to lead from a regulated nervous system. She built the Growth Guide app so the shift lasts long after a session ends.

Her gala package · The Growth Path

Three private breathwork sessions, three personalized Growth Guides, and support between sessions.

kyliecappellano.com ↗@breathworkwithky ↗

Founder · The Capture Corner San Diego

Cameron Martin

Creating memorable experiences and capturing the moments that bring people together. A three-time The Knot Best of Weddings winner and WeddingWire Couples’ Choice Award recipient, and the official photo booth partner of the Impact Launch Gala.
