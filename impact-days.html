How it works

A partnership between equals

You know the need. We bring the people and do the organizing.

First

Tell us what you need

Fill in the form below, or just email. We ask what a genuinely useful day looks like for you — not what is easy for us.

Then

We pick a date together

Published in advance so you can plan around it. We work to your calendar, including your busiest and quietest seasons.

And

We fill the day

Recruiting, scheduling, reminders and turnout are ours. You get a confirmed headcount before the day arrives.

What each side brings

You bring the cause. We bring the room.

Impact brings

  • A room full of working adults who chose to be there
  • All the organizing — recruiting, scheduling, reminders, turnout
  • A date you can plan around, published in advance
  • Skills beyond labor: marketing, finance, operations, design
  • Your organization in front of our members and our audience
  • A recurring relationship, not a one-off day

You bring

  • The cause, and the reason any of it matters
  • The work that genuinely needs doing that day
  • Local knowledge — who needs what, and where
  • Supervision on the day and anything site-specific
  • Honest feedback on whether we were actually useful

What we never ask a partner for

Money. A donation. A sponsorship. Your donor list. An endorsement of Impact, our scholarship, or our gala. Impact Days cost a partner organization nothing, ever. This is our members giving their time, and that is all it is.
